Privacy

Last updated: October 2026.

Flock Rescue is a game in development. This page explains what this website collects.

The game

The browser demo keeps your progress (levels, stars, wool and settings) in your own browser's local storage. It is not sent to us.

This website

If you arrived from one of our ads, we may use the Meta Pixel to measure how many people visit this page and start the demo. Meta may use cookies to do this. You can manage ad preferences in your Facebook or Instagram settings.

If you sign up for launch news, we keep your email address only to tell you when the game launches, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: reply to any email from us, or open an issue on the project's GitHub page.
